Helpful answers

Frequently asked questions

What should I bring to my tax appointment?

Bring photo identification, taxpayer information for everyone on the return, income forms such as W-2s and 1099s, prior-year returns if available, deduction records, and any IRS or state notices. Use the secure portal instead of email for confidential documents.

How long does payroll onboarding take?

Timing depends on employee count, payroll history, pay schedules, tax-account access, and the readiness of employee records. Contact us for an onboarding timeline tailored to your business.

Can you help with IRS or state tax notices?

We can review notices and discuss possible next steps. Please bring or securely upload the full notice and related records. Available representation services depend on the matter and applicable credentials and authorization.

How do I send documents securely?

Do not use regular email for sensitive tax or payroll records. Contact us first, then use the secure portal link provided by the AJ Tax & Payroll team.
